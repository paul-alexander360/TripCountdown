# Travel Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Travel Tracker ("the app") helps you plan trips: trips, to-do lists, itineraries, luggage, tickets and travel links. This policy explains what the app does with information.

## Information stored on your phone
The things you enter (trip names, dates, destinations, to-dos, itinerary notes, luggage, and the photos and PDF files you choose) are stored only on your phone, inside the app. We do not have accounts, we do not upload this information to our servers, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Permissions
- Notifications: used only for the trip and to-do reminders you turn on in Settings.
- Photos and files: the app only receives the pictures and PDF files you pick yourself, and it saves a copy inside the app.

## Advertising
The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and general device information to show ads and measure results. You can learn more in Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and manage ad personalization in your phone's Google settings.

## Links to other websites
The travel resources section opens third-party websites (for example Skyscanner, Booking.com, Discover Cars, Viator and World Nomads) in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Some of these links may become affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission if you make a purchase, at no extra cost to you.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13.
